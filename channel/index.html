Description
What if… you woke up with ten hours to build a zombie-proof home? What if the floating islands were close enough to reach? What if the ruins were still guarding something?

Red Scarf Tales is an animated adventure series. Every episode starts with one question and drops YOU into a bright, painted fantasy world to answer it. Follow the stick-figure hero in the red scarf as he crafts, builds, explores and survives across the Grasslands Chase, the Floating Islands, the Ancient Ruins and Skyfarer's Dusk.
