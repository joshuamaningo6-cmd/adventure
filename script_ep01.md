# Episode 1: What If You Had 10 Hours to Build a Zombie-Proof Home?

Runtime 8:00 (16:9). Narration is second person ("you"), about 940 words at 140–150 wpm (~6.5 min of voice). The rest is silent action.

**Title options**
1. What If You Had 10 Hours to Build a Zombie-Proof Home?
2. What If Zombies Came Every Sunset? (Building From Scratch)
3. Grasslands Chase: Surviving the First Night From Scratch

**Thumbnail:** split down the middle with the Scarf in the centre. Left: sunny meadow, holding a wooden hammer beside a half-built shack. Right: navy night, glowing eyes in the bushes, the shack now a spiked fortress with torches. Top text in bold white and neon green: **10 HOURS TO BUILD**.

**Enemies:** shadow zombies are ragged black silhouettes with glowing orange eyes. The Brute is three times their height with a cracked, glowing chest.
